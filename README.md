# HOODOGE devlog

What is being built across the HOODOGE ecosystem — games, the website and the shared Treats system — summarised as it ships. Code for these projects lives in private repositories; this log is the public record of what changed.

**Live:** [Sherwood Outlaw](https://outlaw.hoodoge.com) (card game) · [Hoodoge Pop](https://pop.hoodoge.com) (puzzle game) · [HOODOGE TV](https://tv.hoodoge.com) (24h ambient scene) · [HOODOGE Website](https://hoodoge.com) (home of the goodest outlaw)

## Recent development

- **Sherwood Outlaw — update** · 2026-10-25  
  Fletch tuning: bigger foes now close in one step a turn, and her starting shot hits harder.
- **Sherwood Outlaw — update** · 2026-10-25  
  Longer maps with more campfires, a bigger heal after each act, and a few card tweaks.
- **Sherwood Outlaw — update** · 2026-10-25  
  Fletch tweaks: one keepsake reworked and the starter Pin card now pins the pack.
- **Sherwood Outlaw — update** · 2026-10-25  
  Engine update: Swift's dives hit harder from height, with a height badge on stage.
- **Sherwood Outlaw — fix** · 2026-10-24  
  Home screen polish: button labels centred and sized, burn button on one line.
- **Sherwood Outlaw — update** · 2026-10-24  
  Home screen redesign: a painted wooden bar with the menu, wallet Treats and run buttons.
- **Sherwood Outlaw — fix** · 2026-10-24  
  Treats window now scrolls on small screens.
- **Sherwood Outlaw — update** · 2026-10-24  
  Get Treats inside the game: burn HOODOGE from the Treats window and a new HOW TO GET TREATS guide.
- **Sherwood Outlaw — update** · 2026-10-24  
  The Shop now opens on top of the outlaw picker when you jump to it from there.
- **Sherwood Outlaw — release** · 2026-10-24  
  Conker the Bombardier is here: a new outlaw who plants acorn bombs instead of swinging, with a full deck of his own and fresh art. Sherwood Club members can pick him up in the Shop.
- **Sherwood Outlaw — update** · 2026-10-22  
  The archer's arrows fly straight even mid-hop, and every one of her new cards now has its own strike.
- **Sherwood Outlaw — update** · 2026-10-22  
  The archer's card pool now matches the hound's, with new shots, footwork and close-quarters tricks, each with its own icon. Gold explains itself and the collection shows cards per outlaw.
