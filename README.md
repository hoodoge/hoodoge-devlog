# HOODOGE devlog

What is being built across the HOODOGE ecosystem — games, the website and the shared Treats system — summarised as it ships. Code for these projects lives in private repositories; this log is the public record of what changed.

**Live:** [Sherwood Outlaw](https://outlaw.hoodoge.com) (roguelike deckbuilder) · [Hoodoge Pop](https://pop.hoodoge.com) (match-3) · [HOODOGE TV](https://tv.hoodoge.com) (24h ambient scene) · [HOODOGE Website](https://hoodoge.com) (home of the goodest outlaw)

## Recent development

- **Sherwood Outlaw — Mobile** · 2026-10-16  
  Landscape-first play on phones and installable as a home-screen app.
- **Sherwood Outlaw — Shop** · 2026-10-16  
  Cosmetic shop opened: combat glow colours and leaderboard titles, paid with Treats. New playable outlaws coming later.
- **Sherwood Outlaw — Leaderboard** · 2026-10-16  
  Win/loss record on the title screen and leaderboard; abandoning a run now asks for confirmation.
- **Sherwood Outlaw — Progression** · 2026-10-15  
  Unlock progression with feats and a Collection screen; Treats rewards for clearing all three acts.
- **Sherwood Outlaw — Card Combat** · 2026-10-15  
  Intent bubbles with move names, attack damage preview, monster dossiers, settings panel, and a new sound set.
- **Sherwood Outlaw — Title Screen** · 2026-10-15  
  Hand-painted plank menu, designer map art for all three acts, and a blurred act backdrop behind the stage.
- **Sherwood Outlaw — Launch** · 2026-10-14  
  Sherwood Outlaw is live: a HOODOGE roguelike deckbuilder across three acts, with a global leaderboard.
- **HOODOGE Website — Tools** · 2026-10-11  
  Stonk Detector moved into the main site alongside the Burn page.
- **HOODOGE Website — Buyback** · 2026-10-10  
  Buyback & burn page live with on-chain history.
- **HOODOGE Treats — Ledger** · 2026-10-01  
  Every HOODOGE product now shares one Treats balance; TV snacks and the Pop shop moved onto it.
- **Hoodoge Pop — Launch** · 2026-09-29  
  Hoodoge Pop is live: a 60-second match-3 with ranked duels, effects and music.
- **HOODOGE TV — Music** · 2026-09-19  
  Original solo-piano soundtrack with a shuffle player and sound toggle.
