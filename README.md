# HOODOGE devlog

What is being built across the HOODOGE ecosystem — games, the website and the shared Treats system — summarised as it ships. Code for these projects lives in private repositories; this log is the public record of what changed.

**Live:** [Sherwood Outlaw](https://outlaw.hoodoge.com) (card game) · [Hoodoge Pop](https://pop.hoodoge.com) (puzzle game) · [HOODOGE TV](https://tv.hoodoge.com) (24h ambient scene) · [HOODOGE Website](https://hoodoge.com) (home of the goodest outlaw)

## Recent development

- **Sherwood Outlaw — update** · 2026-10-21  
  Mystery choices now show their result, card draws are visible, the shopkeeper can be visited again, warnings read clearer, a miss sounds glum, and the collection lists cards per outlaw.
- **Sherwood Outlaw — update** · 2026-10-21  
  Attack strikes stay on screen a little longer before they fade.
- **Sherwood Outlaw — update** · 2026-10-21  
  Story stops now unfold over their full illustration, a dropped card sinks into the monster it was aimed at, and the rest of the hand holds still while you aim.
- **Sherwood Outlaw — update** · 2026-10-21  
  Every attack card now has its own strike: charges throw up dust, bites drip venom, barks ripple out, and the archer's arrows fly across the field before they land.
- **Sherwood Outlaw — update** · 2026-10-21  
  Connecting a wallet from a phone browser now keeps the game where it is: the wallet app only approves and signs, then hands you back.
- **Sherwood Outlaw — update** · 2026-10-21  
  A card you drag onto a monster or your outlaw turns see-through so you can still see what is underneath.
- **Sherwood Outlaw — update** · 2026-10-20  
  Story stops that cost or restore health now show your current and maximum health beside the choices.
- **Sherwood Outlaw — update** · 2026-10-20  
  Every monster now walks in on the new outlaw, being backed into a corner stings the same from start to finish, and the rest-stop preview shows what a reworded improvement does.
- **Sherwood Outlaw — fix** · 2026-10-20  
  Picking the new outlaw right after connecting a wallet works again.
- **Sherwood Outlaw — update** · 2026-10-20  
  The outlaw picker shows the new Club outlaw in full colour before a wallet is connected, with the lock on the badge alone.
- **Sherwood Outlaw — update** · 2026-10-20  
  The second outlaw is open to Sherwood Club members, and the picker now re-reads a wallet's standing instead of trusting an old sign-in.
- **Sherwood Outlaw — update** · 2026-10-20  
  Groundwork for the next outlaw: she keeps her distance, the monsters close in, and her cards and icons are in place behind the coming-soon sign.
