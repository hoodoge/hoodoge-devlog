# HOODOGE devlog

What is being built across the HOODOGE ecosystem — games, the website and the shared Treats system — summarised as it ships. Code for these projects lives in private repositories; this log is the public record of what changed.

**Live:** [Sherwood Outlaw](https://outlaw.hoodoge.com) (card game) · [Hoodoge Pop](https://pop.hoodoge.com) (puzzle game) · [HOODOGE TV](https://tv.hoodoge.com) (24h ambient scene) · [HOODOGE Website](https://hoodoge.com) (home of the goodest outlaw)

## Recent development

- **Sherwood Outlaw — Map** · 2026-10-17  
  The mid-battle map view is back, and the trail markers are smaller and tighter.
- **Sherwood Outlaw — Sound** · 2026-10-17  
  A full sound pack replaces the code-made tones: every tap, swipe, hit and reward has its own layered sounds, and the music dips under the victory and defeat stings.
- **Sherwood Outlaw — Battle** · 2026-10-17  
  The monster introduction now appears at the top of the battle, out of the way of your cards.
- **Sherwood Outlaw — Wallet** · 2026-10-17  
  Disconnect your wallet right from the Shop or the Treats window.
- **Sherwood Outlaw — Treats** · 2026-10-17  
  A Treats window on the title screen: see what you earned, what your wallet holds, and claim with one tap when it qualifies.
- **Sherwood Outlaw — Treats** · 2026-10-17  
  Treats earned in the game now move onto a wallet only for HOODOGE holders above a set amount or Sherwood Origins NFT owners; everything earned stays safe until then, and How to Play explains the rule.
- **Sherwood Outlaw — Art** · 2026-10-17  
  The WANTED poster got its painted look: parchment, lettering, rank seals and a HOODOGE mugshot. The settings gear is easier to spot.
- **Sherwood Outlaw — Progression** · 2026-10-17  
  A new way to grow your collection: every run adds to your WANTED poster, and one shopkeeper now keeps something special under the counter. Stage characters stand closer to the ground.
- **Sherwood Outlaw — Art** · 2026-10-17  
  New painted backdrops for the after-battle and treasure screens, and two keepsake icons redrawn to match the rest.
- **Sherwood Outlaw — Art & Polish** · 2026-10-17  
  HOODOGE now wears the key-art look in battle, a proper gear opens settings from the title screen, and the top bar hints when it has more to show.
- **HOODOGE Website — Roadmap** · 2026-10-17  
  Roadmap updated with Sherwood Outlaw, coming soon.
- **HOODOGE Website — Mascot** · 2026-10-17  
  Hoodoge's arrow now flies forward from his bow when you click him.
