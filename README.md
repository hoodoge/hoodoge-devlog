# HOODOGE devlog

What is being built across the HOODOGE ecosystem — games, the website and the shared Treats system — summarised as it ships. Code for these projects lives in private repositories; this log is the public record of what changed.

**Live:** [Sherwood Outlaw](https://outlaw.hoodoge.com) (card game) · [Hoodoge Pop](https://pop.hoodoge.com) (puzzle game) · [HOODOGE TV](https://tv.hoodoge.com) (24h ambient scene) · [HOODOGE Website](https://hoodoge.com) (home of the goodest outlaw)

## Recent development

- **Sherwood Outlaw — balance** · 2026-10-19  
  A big balance pass on cards so every pick costs more thought, and early fights that no longer repeat the same foe.
- **HOODOGE Website — Play** · 2026-10-19  
  Sherwood Outlaw is now linked from the site, and the roadmap marks it as shipped.
- **Sherwood Outlaw — challenge** · 2026-10-19  
  Tougher mid-run challengers that each push back on a different habit, plus small readability fixes and an art touch-up.
- **Sherwood Outlaw — update** · 2026-10-19  
  A long-term challenge for players who keep winning, more varied monster behaviour, map surprises that can now go wrong, a wide balance pass, and a larger, cleaner layout on phones.
- **Sherwood Outlaw — balance** · 2026-10-18  
  Tester feedback pass: bleed is capped and some foes shrug it off, long fights make every monster angrier, standing behind a wall no longer carries over, and almost every card is open from the first run.
- **Sherwood Outlaw — update** · 2026-10-18  
  How-to-play in eleven languages, a confirm step before taking a reward card, fewer trinkets per run, and a tougher luck draw for the other side.
- **Sherwood Outlaw — polish** · 2026-10-18  
  Two full practice runs on the live game, and the trinket shelf now makes room when it fills up.
- **Sherwood Outlaw — balance & polish** · 2026-10-18  
  Bigger packs that hit softer, a final fight that calls for help, a trader behind some question marks, and a lighter first load.
- **Sherwood Outlaw — Progression** · 2026-10-18  
  Rank bundles now climb in power: early ranks round out your deck, the top ranks hold the strongest cards. The big haul after each act's final fight fits on one page again.
- **Sherwood Outlaw — Balance** · 2026-10-18  
  Resting at the fire now heals less, so sharpening a card is the better call when you are healthy; a few upgrades were evened out as well.
- **Sherwood Outlaw — New cards** · 2026-10-18  
  Twenty-three new cards and twelve new trinkets, each with its own art, including cards that belong to two styles at once.
- **Sherwood Outlaw — Lanes** · 2026-10-18  
  Four deck styles now hold up on their own: Brawler and Chomp got stronger, Zoomies and Guard Dog were trimmed, and the game offers you more of whatever style your deck is leaning toward.
