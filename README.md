# HOODOGE devlog

What is being built across the HOODOGE ecosystem — games, the website and the shared Treats system — summarised as it ships. Code for these projects lives in private repositories; this log is the public record of what changed.

**Live:** [Sherwood Outlaw](https://outlaw.hoodoge.com) (card game) · [Hoodoge Pop](https://pop.hoodoge.com) (puzzle game) · [HOODOGE TV](https://tv.hoodoge.com) (24h ambient scene) · [HOODOGE Website](https://hoodoge.com) (home of the goodest outlaw)

## Recent development

- **Sherwood Outlaw — New cards** · 2026-10-18  
  Twenty-three new cards and twelve new trinkets, each with its own art, including cards that belong to two styles at once.
- **Sherwood Outlaw — Lanes** · 2026-10-18  
  Four deck styles now hold up on their own: Brawler and Chomp got stronger, Zoomies and Guard Dog were trimmed, and the game offers you more of whatever style your deck is leaning toward.
- **Sherwood Outlaw — Fixes** · 2026-10-18  
  The battle screen no longer shifts sideways and cuts off its left edge, and tapping the hand right as a fight ends is handled quietly.
- **Sherwood Outlaw — Fixes** · 2026-10-18  
  A run no longer starts silently when the server cannot be reached; a clear message asks you to try again. Rest-stop choices now fit the screen and the bounty strip is readable.
- **Sherwood Outlaw — Endgame** · 2026-10-18  
  Beating the Bear King now ends with a single draw that adds a luck card to your deck for good, and the score screen shows the Treats you earned, now two per gold coin left.
- **Sherwood Outlaw — Balance** · 2026-10-18  
  The starting luck deck is calmer now: mostly plain cards with a single plus one, so the earned cards from the Bear King matter more. The tutorial also explains the luck card after your first attack.
- **Sherwood Outlaw — Settings** · 2026-10-18  
  Music and sound effect volume sliders, pinch-zoom on the map, and lighter backgrounds for phones that were crashing in Safari.
- **Sherwood Outlaw — Combat** · 2026-10-18  
  The luck pile now carries over between fights, the drawn card flips open large before the attack lands, and a top-right pill shows cards left out of the whole deck.
- **Sherwood Outlaw — Progress** · 2026-10-17  
  Your growth now follows your wallet: connect it on any device and your luck deck, poster and collection are all there, with nothing lost.
- **Sherwood Outlaw — Luck Deck** · 2026-10-17  
  Every attack now draws a luck card, for you and for the monsters, and winning the final fight grows your deck for good. Chests let you choose, and a first balance pass evens out the four play styles.
- **Sherwood Outlaw — Flow** · 2026-10-17  
  After a win, picking your card takes you straight back to the map. How to Play now explains the card badges.
- **Sherwood Outlaw — Map** · 2026-10-17  
  The mid-battle map view is back, and the trail markers are smaller and tighter.
