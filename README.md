# HOODOGE devlog

What is being built across the HOODOGE ecosystem — games, the website and the shared Treats system — summarised as it ships. Code for these projects lives in private repositories; this log is the public record of what changed.

**Live:** [Sherwood Outlaw](https://outlaw.hoodoge.com) (card game) · [Hoodoge Pop](https://pop.hoodoge.com) (puzzle game) · [HOODOGE TV](https://tv.hoodoge.com) (24h ambient scene) · [HOODOGE Website](https://hoodoge.com) (home of the goodest outlaw)

## Recent development

- **Sherwood Outlaw — fix** · 2026-10-20  
  Picking the new outlaw right after connecting a wallet works again.

  The outlaw picker shows the new Club outlaw in full colour before a wallet is connected, with the lock on the badge alone.

  The phone menu now scrolls, so every link and the theme switch are reachable on smaller screens.
- **Sherwood Outlaw — update** · 2026-10-20  
  Two of the upcoming Sherwood Club outlaws got their redrawn portraits, and the victory screen now fits on short phone screens.
- **Sherwood Outlaw — update** · 2026-10-20  
  The outlaw picker now shows which newcomers are for club members and lets you connect or disconnect your wallet right there.
- **Sherwood Outlaw — roster** · 2026-10-20  
  Three new outlaws step onto the roster with their portraits, marked coming soon while their cards are forged.

  One of the trinkets now also keeps you out of surprise fights, so it earns its slot.
